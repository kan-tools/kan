1. NORTH STAR (frozen north-star.json only)
telos/raw-data-and-projections (Decision bafyreied3rklhs57...): "Raw attested data is retained in full and
never collapsed in the store. Every simplification ... is a determined projection computed on demand over
that raw data ... A field holding a computed value is the failure mode." It is in recorded tension with
telos/performance-at-scale (InTensionWith, both directions). The telos deliberately declares no witnesses.
I did not add one.
How each scope serves it:
  A: the physical reader is a projection over retained raw bundle bytes. It must neither drop a legitimately
     retained raw state (an empty original) nor accept a non-retained one.
  B: assembly is a disposable projection over caller-selected raw frames. It must retain every input and every
     lineage, and must not collapse duplicates, conflicts, holes or claims into synthetic completeness.
Tension in play: both components trade bounded budgets (performance) against retaining every raw item. Both
refuse wholesale on budget exhaustion rather than silently truncating. That is the correct side of the
tension. Scope B's derived booleans (unconfirmed_claim, missing_start, events_after_terminal) are projections
whose raw values stay reachable through Assembly.observations. They are not stored fields, so they do not hit
the telos' failure mode.
