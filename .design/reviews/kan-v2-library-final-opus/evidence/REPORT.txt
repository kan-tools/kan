3b. Active capture interval vs. proof of an actual SQLite wait — kept distinct

wall-clock span. That is NOT proof that capture waited. My instrumented faithful
copy (probes/zz_review_p4_probe.rs) records what PRODUCTION returned:

  Every later window that won overlapped an admission that waited (later_waited ==

3e. Cleanup when capture panics (probe, logs/51)
