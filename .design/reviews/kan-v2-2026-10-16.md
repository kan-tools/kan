The canonical re-encoding check then rejects valid manifests/captures. Reproduced cases include slots, operation tokens and payload hashes beginning `00 01 55 12 00`; the independent Python encoder accepts them. This is rejection of valid evidence, not an observed loss from any live v2 archive. No live archive has been imported.
