   - `sealed_record_refusals_do_not_rewrite_control` only reaches the `sealed_for_capacity` filter (`:378`). Removing that filter alone also survives; removing both together is killed.
   - Only my probe kills the guard mutant. It shows that, without the guard, a later worker `sqlite_full` overwrites the first `attempt_limit` reason with a durable write. That overwritten reason is also a small instance of the telos tension.
2. **Low-medium: older v2 journals are reopened for writing.** A journal the old binary sealed for `session_limit` (reserve 0) accepts new attempt writes under the new binary (probe: `record=Ok`, reserve 0).

   - They now record with no reserve, and an old binary pointed at the same file would still refuse. This needs an explicit decision or disclosure.
