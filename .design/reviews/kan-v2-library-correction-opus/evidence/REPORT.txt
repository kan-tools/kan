## Verdict: **BLOCK** (narrow; one hunk)

The five test pins, the retry-styling fix, the docs and the reversion trailer all hold up when executed. I reproduced each one and every kill is a named, compiled assertion. The blocker is the correction's CI fix in `tests/evidence_capture_contention.rs:308-327`. It sets `busy_timeout(Duration::ZERO)` and skips busy windows, but keeps `assert!(held > 0)` at `:341`. Capture holds SQLite's write lock at roughly 92–100% of the writer's attempt instants, so the writer often gets no window at all. Measured locally: the real P4 test failed `assertion failed: held > 0` in **6 of 35 runs** (5/12, then 1/20, then 0/3 in suite runs). The instrumented copy got zero windows in 3 of 10. The pre-correction helper (HEAD^) passed **15/15** locally. The correction replaces a Linux-only `DatabaseBusy` panic with a cross-platform flake in a test that CI runs (`ci.yml:34`). CI's original 5.54 s failure shows the writer was already starved for its full 5 s wait on Linux. Inference, not verified: a zero-wait writer is at least as likely to get `held == 0` there. Production is unaffected. However, a required CI job that passes or fails on scheduler luck invites rerun-until-green, and that defeats the evidence discipline this repo depends on. Per ADR-52, the fix for this hunk gets its own review round. Nothing else in the correction needs rework to clear this BLOCK. The follow-ups listed below are separate.

The helper's question, answered directly. When windows happen (1–6 per run), they fall inside the capture span (230–1225 ms within 0–1245 ms). Every one of 45+ runs had complete capture (`observed == queued`, 87/88). So the helper shows *real but sparse and nondeterministic* overlap. The printed "~9% duty" is false: measured held duty is 0–2.3% (≤6 × 5 ms over ~1.3 s).
