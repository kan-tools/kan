2. NORTH STAR (quoted from the supplied north-star.json, not paraphrased)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
telos/raw-data-and-projections — "Raw data retained, simplifications projected":
  "Raw attested data is retained in full and never collapsed in the store. Every simplification --
   identity, status, confidence, tension, ordering -- is a determined projection computed on demand over
   that raw data, parameterised by a base the viewer chooses, and therefore swappable … A field holding a
   computed value is the failure mode: it asserts an output of the fold as if it were input, and it
   forecloses every other projection."
On record it is InTensionWith telos/performance-at-scale. The telos also records that it deliberately
declares no witnesses: "day witnesses the attainment, CI enforces the maintenance."
I read no live log, as Git-first requires. Live-ness was therefore not re-checked against `kan show`; it
is the supplied file's content.

How this work serves the telos. Publication copies the original raw records, proofs, plan and membership
byte-exactly into the output. It derives lookup columns only from those authenticated bytes. It never
deletes or overwrites a bundle, a failed output or prior history. It records publication-failed as an
attributed unsuccessful check rather than as a computed "absent" fact. It treats the signed O1 output
commitment, not a filename, as the output's identity. I verified each of these by execution (§4).

Tension with performance-at-scale. Publication re-acquires and re-replays the entire bundle three times
and the output twice, and the acquisition budget is cumulative (P18 needs about 5–6× a single inspection
pass). That is correctness bought with work, which is the right side of the tension for a recovery path.
Its cost is undocumented (follow-up F-PUB-6).
