## Findings

1. **Frontier and lineage rejections are untested (medium).** The design says "A regressed queue frontier or mismatched stored binding fails reconciliation." Three mutants survive, each failing only on the lock flake from finding 3 or not at all:
   - D: removing `q.next > queue.next`.
   - I: removing the same-`next`/different-`previous` check.
   - F: removing the "ack beyond acked frontier" rejection (`src/evidence_operations.rs:411-419`), which passed everything.

   Also, once a check like this fires, the worker can never write to the journal again, and nothing tells the operator why. The dashboard just shows "stale" indefinitely.
2. **Reporter future-clock guard is untested (low).** Removing `r.updated_at>Date.now()/1000+5` (`web/operations/app.js:31`) passes all tests. Only the worker's future-clock case is covered.
3. **New CI flake risk (medium).** The full `evidence_operations` suite panicked with `DatabaseBusy` at `tests/evidence_operations.rs:145`/`:483` (an `.unwrap()` on journal open/snapshot) in 5 of 11 runs where locking was unchanged. The 11 are the 6 plain reviewed-code runs plus 5 mutants that don't touch locking (failures: 1 plain, D, G twice, I).
   - With the two new tests skipped: 0 of 10 failures on reviewed code, 0 of 8 on HEAD source (`flake-*.txt`).
   - The likely trigger is extra concurrent fsync load (`fullfsync=ON`) against the 100 ms busy timeout. That's unconfirmed, and Linux CI may be less affected.
4. **Worker badge contradicts the notice (low).** When `sampled_at` is in the future or not a number, the badge reads `idle` while the notice says "missing or stale" (`probes/ui_probe.out`). The cause is that `age=Math.max(0,…)` and a NaN age both get past the `age>45` check at `app.js:19,23`.
5. **Past interruptions are mixed into current status (design follow-up).** A single unclean session, even one 30 days old, makes "Status is current" unreachable forever. That includes the case where it falls outside the 100-row window, through `unseen`. Interrupted sessions also don't appear in the new coverage-history panel (`probes/ui_probe.out`). This matches the design's wording ("stale … unclosed sessions remain uncertain"), so it's not a code bug. But after any crash, the current-vs-historical separation stops working. Graceful SIGTERM does close sessions (`src/bin/kan-v2.rs:915,925`), so this only happens after crashes or SIGKILL.
6. **Catalog category is a forced fit, and nothing enforces the storage rule (low).** The browser `localStorage` row is filed as `source=system-config` because the enum has no browser category. Surface conformance only checks that the row is well-formed. Nothing scans `web/` for storage writes, so "only `kan.theme` is persisted" is enforced only by the Node theme test.
