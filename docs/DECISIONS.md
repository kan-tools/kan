# kan — decisions moved

Architecture Decision Records now live as individual files under
[`adrs/`](../adrs/README.md). The format, lifecycle, reconstruction rules, and
relationship to Requests for Comments are defined by
[RFC 0000](../rfcs/0000-rfc-and-adr-process.md).

ADR-1 through ADR-91 were migrated without silently filling historical gaps.
Their original prose is retained in each ADR's `Historical record` section and
verified by `scripts/check-rfcs-adrs.sh`.
